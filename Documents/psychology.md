## Defense Mechanisms 

Defense Mechanisms are ways our minds try to protect us from anxiety or threats to self-esteem.

In the game, the player is able to set a "Playbook". Unfournately as is in reality, a playbook is only partially. Life and events happen, and we often regress from guidelines. 

At some point in a V2 or V3 iterating of the game, we will introduce hidden defense mechanisms to players.

A defense mechanism is a hidden unconscious behavioural pattern that the player has but is not aware the character has.

These defense mechanisms will be randomly trigerred at a cadense, say once a week, and cause the character to perform poorly at some tasks. 

This will lead the character down to investigate concepts such as "shadow work" and "past reflection" to understand their past, and how they developed these defense mechanisms.

Once they complete a shadow work course, the character will unlock the "subconscious mind", boosting karma by 1000 points, enabling new capbilities such as editing the subconscious brain, rewriting how defense mechanisms work.

Unlocking the subconscious brain will be a new capability. 

The idea of the game: only users who have been screened to be interested and investigate why their character doesn't perform at 100% will be able to reach this place. 


## Spiral Dynamics

Spiral Dynamics is a psychological model for human evolution.
The model maps how values, worldviews, and consciousness evolve through 8 distinct color-coded stages.

In the game, societies are ranked into distinct stages:
**Stage Purple**: Tribal societies existing in pure natural 
**Stage Red**: Societies with crimes, gangs, fighting, violence.  
**Stage Blue**: Societies with distinct religion belief, rules must be followed strictly by the society. 
**Stage Orange**: Societies where people primarily care about profit-maximization and gaining more money, materialism, and consumerism. 
**Stage Green**: Societies where people value relationships, connection, the environment, animals, and human rights. 
**Stage Yellow**: Societies where people understand the spiral, understand the geographical map, and deal with global problems.  

In the game, each city will be mapped into a distinct stage. For the first few years of playing, societies will mostly be either Purple, Red, Blue, Orange, Green, or Yellow.

## Biases

A character many have a given biases, hindering their performance.  

These biases can be:
--> Gender Bias: Consider one gender better than another. 
--> Availability Heuristic: Using information that comes to mind quickly 
--> Self-Serving Bias: Attributing personal successes to internal traits and failures to external factors.

Later in the game, a player may "unlock" the bias calculator, which shows how biased we've detected that their character is through completion of challenges.

Bias is calculated through behaviour the player exhibits in challenges.

## Onboarding 

In onboarding, the system will ask the user a few questions. The questions are not meant to sound too personal, but the goal is to make them some heuristics that will determine:
- The initial environment the player spawns in 
- What "hidden traumas" we assign to this character 
- What "hidden anger factor" we assign to the character
- What is their attachment style
